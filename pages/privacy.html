Last Updated: January 19, 2026

At MeritBoard, accessible from meritboard.com, one of our main priorities is the privacy of our visitors. This Privacy Policy document contains types of information that is collected and recorded by MeritBoard and how we use it.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information to provide better services to all our users. This includes:

  • Personal Information: When you register via third-party services (Google, Facebook, Zoho), we may collect your Name, Email Address, and Profile Picture.
  • Usage Data: We collect data on how you interact with our quizzes, including scores, time spent, and pages visited.
  • Log Files: Standard internet log information such as IP address, browser type, and timestamp.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect in the following ways:

  • To personalize your learning experience and track your quiz progress.
  • To send you emails regarding updates, new mock tests, or educational newsletters.
  • To analyze website traffic and improve our website functionality.
  • To prevent fraud and maintain the security of our platform.

3. Cookies and Web Beacons

Like any other website, MeritBoard uses 'cookies'. These cookies are used to store information including visitors' preferences, and the pages on the website that the visitor accessed or visited. The information is used to optimize the users' experience by customizing our web page content based on visitors' browser type and/or other information.

We may employ third-party companies and individuals due to the following reasons:

  • Authentication: We use services like Google, Facebook, and Zoho for secure login.

Note that MeritBoard has no access to or control over these cookies that are used by third-party advertisers.

We value your trust in providing us your Personal Information, thus we are striving to use commercially acceptable means of protecting it.
