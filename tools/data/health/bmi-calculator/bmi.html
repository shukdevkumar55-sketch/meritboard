Understanding Body Mass Index (BMI)

Body Mass Index (BMI) is a medical screening tool that estimates body fat by comparing height to weight. Our tool uses official WHO standards.

The Formula

Metric: kg / m²
Imperial: 703 × lbs / in²

WHO Categories

18.5 - 24.9: Healthy
25.0 - 29.9: Overweight

Medical Disclaimer: This tool is for informational purposes only. Muscle mass and bone density are not considered.
